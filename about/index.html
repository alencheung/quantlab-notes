about the desk

An independent desk that publishes its own scorecard.

QuantLab Research Notes is the public surface of a one-desk quantitative research platform. Every investigation shows its data, its method and its blind spots — and every falsifiable claim becomes a timestamped, machine-graded standing call. It is written for allocators, researchers and collaborators evaluating the work.

How the numbers work

  1. Statistics are pure code. Prose interprets, never invents: every number on this site regenerates from one analysis script per note, over auditable house data.
  2. Calls are timestamped and graded by code. Every falsifiable line the desk publishes becomes a standing call; a deterministic grader scores it the moment its window matures — and a graded call is never revised.
  3. An editor gate before publication. Each note passes an adversarial editor-role review that checks every quoted number against the machine output; a note that fails the gate stays a draft.
  4. Tamper-evident records. Results append to a hash-chained, append-only ledger; the chain refuses to extend if any entry is altered after the fact.

What is live and what is backtest

The strategy-library results on the hub are full-sample backtests, net of modeled trading costs. The live book is paper-forward: it trades in simulation on real-time data, writes every session to a public hash-chained ledger, and carries a mechanical evidence clock that states how much live evidence the book still owes before its backtest statistics can transfer to capital. No client money is managed. The Live Desk series reports the ledger session by session.
